About

Sivan is a Berlin based filmmaker and multidisciplinary artist. Her work moves between directing, editing, curation, and production. A graduate of the full directing track at Sam Spiegel Film & Television School (2024), she wrote and directed two independent short films that went on to screen at festivals including TLVFest and the Haifa International Film Festival. Alongside her own directing work, she edits short fiction, documentary, and live performance content, and curates and produces screenings and events.
