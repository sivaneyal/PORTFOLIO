Analytics

This site uses Umami Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics tool. Umami does not use cookies, does not collect personally identifiable information, and does not track visitors across other websites.

It only records aggregate data such as page views, referral sources, and an approximate visitor country derived from IP address, without storing the IP address itself. No individual visitor profiles are created. See Umami's own privacy policy for full details.

Contact and Password Requests

If you reach out through the Request Password button on a project, or by emailing Sivan directly, the email address and message content you provide are used only to respond to your inquiry. This information is not shared with any third party.

These platforms operate independently of this site, and any data they collect falls under their own terms, not this policy.
